About Us

Rooted in Ceylon Heritage

Pearl Blend is brought to you by S M J Ventures, a Sri Lankan company devoted to sharing the true taste of Ceylon with every household.

Our leaves are sourced from the lush tea estates of Maskeliya, nestled in the cool, misty hills of Sri Lanka's central highlands. Here, rich soil, high rainfall and skilled hands come together to produce authentic, high grade Ceylon black tea with remarkable depth and aroma.

Every pack is carefully packaged and distributed from Maharagama, so the freshness of the estate reaches your cup exactly as it should.
